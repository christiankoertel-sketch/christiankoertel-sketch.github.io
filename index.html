Adam personal mail

"Adam personal mail" is a private tool used only by its owner, Christian Koertel, to read and organise his own Gmail inbox. It is not offered to anyone else.

Privacy policy

  • It accesses only the owner's own Gmail account, with the owner's consent.
  • Email data is used only to help the owner sort, label, archive and review his own email.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
